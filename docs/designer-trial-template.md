# Designer trial — blank record

Copy this form to a private evidence location. Do not commit filled client/reviewer records here.
Human judgments and owner decisions remain blank until the actual person supplies them. For an AI
rehearsal mark **AI technical rehearsal** and record observations separately, without assigning human
rubric scores or risk acceptance. [Guide](operator-guide.md) · [rubric](verification.md#designer-rubric).
